Tinan leNa'vi: learn Na'vi, the language of Avatar

Loading the app…

A free app to learn and practise Na'vi, the language Paul Frommer created for the Avatar films. 14 lessons take you from the sounds of the language to holding a conversation: 59 grammar rules, 247 words and 608 exercises. Learn each lesson in short steps, four words at a time, then practise with flashcards, multiple choice, typed answers and forms to build, with spaced review. When you make a mistake, Tinan shows the expected answer and the rule, then files it under one of 69 error patterns until you master it. No account and nothing to install: your progress stays in your browser. In English and French.

The 14 lessons

  1. The basics
  2. Verbs and infixes
  3. Pronouns
  4. Transitive and intransitive
  5. Adjectives
  6. Adpositions
  7. Questions (Yes/no questions and negation; Who, what, where, when?; Why, how, and answers)
  8. Lenition
  9. The subjunctive and subordinate clauses (The subjunctive and modal verbs; Subordinate clauses and conditions)
  10. Numbers in base 8 (Digits and powers of eight; Building numbers, counting things; Ordinals, fractions and times)
  11. Adverbs and negation (Where ke goes, the ke- prefix; Negative words and double negation; Adverbs)
  12. Participles (Participles; The gerund)
  13. Formal and colloquial registers (Honorific pronouns and the vocative; Ceremonial ‹uy› and when to use it; The colloquial register)
  14. Conversation (Greetings and getting to know someone; Politeness and not understanding; Talking about Na'vi, dialogues)

Sourced grammar

Every rule shows its source: Horen Lì'fyayä leNa'vi, the reference grammar; the LearnNavi dictionary; and Na'viteri, Paul Frommer's blog. Each word links to the Reykunyu dictionary to hear its pronunciation.
